# Cinema artwork

Bundled, generated illustrations used by the overlay; no remote image requests at runtime.

- `cinema-room.png`: transparent cutaway interior, blank illuminated marquee, wall lights and shadows. The five customizable wall posters, marquee lettering, people and seats are not baked into this image. The tiny decorative plaque beside the entrance is part of the facade.
- `cinema-posters.png`: original artwork retained as a source atlas for the five wall posters and their frames. Only the polygonal poster crops are rendered; the rest of this atlas is not used as the room background. `js/decor.js` defines independent cached objects and their placement. Each can be replaced with a local image or hidden through `CINEMA_CONFIG.posters`.
- `cinema-chair.png`: transparent velvet chair, sampled into a cached seat layer with a floor contact shadow.
- `viewers.png`, `viewers-extra.png`: two transparent sheets, each with four character rows and three pose columns (front, walking back, seated).

`js/art.js` contains the source crop rectangles, native canvas caches, facade occlusion mask, lighting positions and stable wardrobe variants. The composition renders in 1920×1080. Movement uses logical coordinates from `js/layout.js`; its projection is calibrated against this room artwork. When replacing an image, update that calibration and inspect both seated and walking viewers, including the entrance and a full hall.

Marquee lettering is generated by `js/decor.js` from `signText` (or `?sign=`), after loading the bundled font. Its measured glyph bounds fit a fixed canvas, which is projected onto the marquee. The decorated facade is shared by the background and foreground occlusion layer, preventing the foreground from covering custom text.
